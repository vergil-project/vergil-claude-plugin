## Features

- **sweep the epic for review findings that were never tracked (#804)**
Adds a step before drafting that reads each task's agentic-review summary (or its round comments when there is no summary), its other comments and PR reviews, and the planning issue's design-review comments, and resolves every untracked problem as found, filed with vrg-intake-create, or dropped by the human. Security-class problems go only through vrg-security-record. Only trusted authors' comments count as records or rulings. The result goes in retrospective section 4. Refs vergil-project/vergil-claude-plugin#760.

- **add the agentic-review gate, and list paused and split-out tasks in epic-implement (#805)**
