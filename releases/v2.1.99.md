  Since the release lock's phase 1, vrg-git refuses a plain push to an open develop PR's branch; every push to an open PR goes through pr-watch's vrg-submit-pr --push. Step 4 and the force-push paragraph now say they cover a branch with no PR, and name the open-PR path.

- **hand every tracked-file change to the implementer writer (#1439)**
  Add a section on the writer: the rule and its two driver exceptions, the dispatch, the driver's check of the result, the one Sonnet re-dispatch, the resume sort with its driver-work marker, the writer's notes and the human-run path. The helper paragraph becomes the helper model rule, and Implement, Preflight step 6, the rebase section and Batch mode now route through the writer. Refs #1432.
- **route merge-review's fix step and pr-watch's fixes through the writer under an issue-implement driver (#1447)**
  Merge review's Fix and check and pr-watch take a writer input from their caller. With a writer (an issue-implement driver), the driver makes and validates security fixes and resolves rebase conflicts itself, under the driver-work marker, and every other code fix goes to vergil:implementer. Without one, the caller's session fixes as before. issue-implement passes the input to both. Ref #1433.
- **name the model of every other helper dispatch, and pin design-review delegates to Opus (#1448)**
  epic-implement, issue-deploy and issue-validate apply the helper model rule; issue-validate's skill-under-test subagent runs on the skill's validation alias from the installed model map; the paad:paad-delegate dispatch passes model opus in epic-create and the paad review doctrine. Ref #1434.
