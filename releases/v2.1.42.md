## Features

- **route deferrals to the deferred queue in intake-review, epic-create and issue-implement (#733)**
Defer in intake-review now moves the item into the deferred queue and requires --reason and --revisit-when; leaving an item in intake is the no-route outcome. epic-create accepts a deferred issue as an entry point and points deferral at the defer route. issue-implement refuses an item under an epic + deferred parent in preflight. intake-capture, the skills index and CLAUDE.md describe the third queue. Part of vergil-project/.github#508.

- **add the deferred-review skill (#734)**
New deferred-review skill (Task 6 of epic vergil-project/.github#508). It lists the deferred queue with vrg-queue deferred, re-checks each item's Revisit when: conditions against the current state, and recommends keep, refresh, revive (intake, planning, or one-off) or drop in batches of 4, applied with vrg-intake-route. Adds its row and section to the skills index.

- **add planning-review skill to pick what to plan next (#735)**
Ranks the planning queue with the human, surfaces in-progress items, and hands picks to epic-create; defers, drops, or leaves the rest via vrg-intake-route. Core rule: plan an epic only when you mean to implement it soon. Task 7 of vergil-project/.github#508.
